Our Myotherapy Services Near Berwick

Muscle tension and joint stiffness can make work, training and everyday jobs harder than they need to be. At Your Body Hub in Officer, our myotherapists assess how you move, where you feel discomfort and what your days involve. We then talk you through the options that suit your needs and preferences.

Treatment may include soft tissue techniques such as trigger point therapy, deep tissue massage, cupping or stretching, along with exercises you can continue at home. We explain each step, ask for your consent and review your response at every visit, adjusting your plan to your goals.

Myotherapy Support for Muscle and Joint Discomfort

We look at how muscle and joint discomfort affects your work, sport and daily routine, then build a treatment plan around the activities that matter most to you.

  • Muscle and Joint Assessment

    We assess your posture, movement and areas of tenderness, then explain our findings and the treatment options that may suit you.

  • Neck and Shoulder Care

    We assess tension around the neck and shoulders and consider hands-on techniques alongside stretches for turning, reaching and sitting at a desk.

  • Back and Hip Care

    We look at back and hip discomfort in the context of your work and activity, then choose treatment and advice based on our assessment.

  • Sport and Training Support

    We assess muscle discomfort linked to training and discuss soft tissue treatment, load changes and exercises to support your return to activity.

  • Home Exercise Guidance

    We show you stretches and exercises to practise between sessions, so you can keep working on movement around your daily routine.

  • Treatment Reviews

    We check how you responded to each session, review changes in your symptoms and movement and adjust your plan to suit your preferences.

Frequently Asked Questions About Myotherapy in Berwick

How far is Your Body Hub from Berwick?

We are at Suite 26, 445 Princes Highway, Officer, a 5 to 10 minute drive from Berwick. The clinic is easy to reach via Princes Highway and there is free on-site parking.

Do I need a referral to see a myotherapist?

No, you do not need a referral to see a myotherapist. You can book directly online or call us on (03) 8578 6544 and our reception team will help you find a time.

How long is a myotherapy appointment?

Appointments are generally 60 minutes. Depending on how many areas of the body need attention, 45 minute and 30 minute appointments are also available. We can help you choose the right length when you book.

Can I claim myotherapy on my health insurance?

Yes, if you have relevant extras cover. We have a HICAPS machine onsite to process your claim on the spot, and you pay any gap on the day. Please note not all private health insurance companies are registered with HICAPS.

What techniques might my myotherapist use?

Depending on your assessment, we may use trigger point therapy, dry needling, deep tissue massage, cupping or passive and active stretching. We may also give you exercises or posture advice to continue at home. We explain each option before we start.

When is the clinic open?

We are open seven days: Monday to Thursday 9am to 8pm, Friday 9am to 7pm, Saturday 9am to 5pm and Sunday 10am to 4pm. These hours make it easier for Berwick residents to book around work and family.
